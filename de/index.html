Listening task — German narration

Takes about 10 minutes. Please use headphones.

What this is. You will hear 1 recordings of the same short documentary passage in German. They are read by different voices. We need a native speaker's judgement on how they sound.

What we are looking for. We are choosing a narrator for historical documentary films in German. The voice has to sound genuinely native — like someone from your country, not a foreigner reading German well. It has to be calm and captivating: the kind of voice you would happily follow through a whole film. It must not be boring, flat or tiring. Please judge the clips against exactly that.

  1. Listen to each clip all the way through before scoring it.
  2. Score every clip on accent and naturalness, and note any word, name or date that is pronounced wrongly.
  3. At the end, rank the clips and tell us which one you would pick.
  4. Press Generate my answers and send back the text that appears.

There is no "right" answer we are hoping for — the clips genuinely differ in quality, and telling us that one is bad is as useful as telling us one is good.
